# AI Learning Agent — Prompt Cookbook 📖⚡

Kumpulan template prompt siap pakai untuk NotebookLM, ChatGPT, Claude, atau Hermes Agent:

---

## 1. Turn a Concept into a 3D Visual (CAD / Robotics)
> *"I want to build a robotics project, but I'm not sure where to start. Give me 3 ideas in an interactive presentation. Pitch them to me, tell me which one is best and why I should build it, and make a 3D model of it (OpenSCAD or interactive Three.js HTML) so I can see it."*

---

## 2. Wire Your Hardware (Pinout & Wiring Guide)
> *(Attach a photo or list of your boards)*  
> *"Make me an interactive presentation showing me how to wire this STM32 board to the ST-LINK programmer, step by step, so I can flash firmware and take power readings safely. Include a pinout connection table and what each pin does."*

---

## 3. Filter the Syllabus & Reading List (Curriculum Sieve)
> *(Paste syllabus & reading list)*  
> *"Here's my module syllabus and reading list. Apply the 80/20 rule: Which books and which specific chapters are actually worth my time, considering my curriculum and the exams I will sit? Which tutorial problems are high-yield and worth doing? Tell me what to completely skip."*

---

## 4. Filter Research Papers (Lit-Review Triage)
> *(Paste title/abstract of 5 papers)*  
> *"I found these 5 papers. I want to learn more about [topic]. Which one is the best single paper to read first, which one is the most legit/methodologically sound, and which ones can I ignore for now? Give me a 3-bullet summary of the core insight."*

---

## 5. Find the 20% That Gets You Unstuck (7-Day Deadline Mode)
> *"I'm stuck on [topic/task] for my coursework and the deadline is in 7 days. What is the absolute minimum 20% I need to understand to attempt and complete it? Skip all history, proofs, and general theory. Give me 1 actionable task I can finish in the next 30 minutes."*

---

## 6. Find the Best Free Resources (Sub-Agent Web Hunter)
> *"Search for the best free resources for [project name] (3D CAD models, open datasets, production codebases, and step-by-step tutorials). Return exact links, license types (MIT/Apache/CC-BY), and why each is directly useful. Highlight any potential gotchas."*

---

## 7. Inspect Repository & Give Immediate Task (Codebase Onboarding)
> *(Provide GitHub repo URL or folder path)*  
> *"Inspect this repository. Don't give me a generic theory lesson. Tell me: What does it do, what is currently broken or unfinished, what is the single most important next task, and explain the exact 20% concept I need to write that code right now."*
